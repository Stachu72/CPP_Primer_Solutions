# Exercise 1.24
> Test the previous program by giving multiple transactions representing multiple ISBNs. The records for each ISBN should be grouped together.

**Mentioned program:** [1.23](https://github.com/Stachu72/CPP_Primer_Solutions/blob/main/Chapter%201/01.23.md)

## Solution

- Input:
```c++
0-201-78345-X 5 20.00
0-201-78345-X 2 22.00
0-201-78345-X 1 15.00
0-201-78632-X 1 75.00
0-201-75659-X 10 10.00
0-201-75659-X 5 12.50
```

- Output:
```c++
The 0-201-78345-X isbn number has appeared 3 times.
The 0-201-78632-X isbn number has appeared 1 times.
The 0-201-75659-X isbn number has appeared 2 times.
```
