# Exercise 2.26
> Which of the following are legal? For those that are illegal, explain why.

(a) `const int buf;` <br>
(b) `int cnt = 0;` <br>
(c) `const int sz = cnt;` <br>
(d) `++cnt; ++sz;`

## Solution

(a) The `const` variable must be initialized - invalid, <br>
(b) valid, <br>
(c) valid, <br>
(d) `++cnt` is valid, `++sz` is invalid - you **cannot** change `const` variables after initialization.
